# Shorten

Write only the sentences readers need to make their next decision or take their next step. The more there is to read, the more the needed information gets buried.

- Leave out reasons and reassurances that do not change what the reader does.
- Leave out what readers might find out anyway when they need it.
- Put each piece of information where it belongs and where readers need it.
- Write information that may change in one place only, and refer to it elsewhere in general terms.
- Call each thing by the same name everywhere readers meet it.
